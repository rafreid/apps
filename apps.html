MoDoMiNo

Jwèt domino mo Kreyòl

Syllable tiles on a domino table. A Kreyòl definition is read aloud, and you build the word one syllable at a time. You can play solo or against others over the shared Palefò broker.

Kòb Pa'm

Pale kòb ou, tande chanj lan

A Haitian currency converter you can talk to. Gourdes against the US dollar, euro, Canadian dollar or Dominican peso at the BRH reference rate. Say an amount and it fills in the figures and reads the answer back. Ask a question about the exchange and the Palefò Assistant answers aloud, in Kreyòl.

FotoLekti

Pran foto, koute an Kreyòl

Snap a photo of any text, a sign, a letter, a menu, a form, and FotoLekti reads it aloud in Kreyòl. One Palefò call does the OCR, the translation and the voice, with read-along highlighting as it speaks. Built for the phone, works offline as an installed app.
